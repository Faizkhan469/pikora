The first keyframe controls the clip's appearance at its trimmed start. The second controls its appearance at its trimmed end. Position is measured as a percentage of the video frame. Presets fill in the controls; press Apply Clip Settings to save them.

To split: play or seek the selected clip to the desired position, pause, then press Split.

5. Editing Tips

• Drag timeline clips to reorder them.

• Video and audio are displayed on separate tracks.

• Use timeline zoom to adjust the visible scale.

• The music waveform is generated in your browser.

• Select a thumbnail to preview and edit that clip.

• Use the visual trim sliders or time fields.

• Split the clip at the current preview position.

• Animate text and video clips between two keyframes.

• Export is processed on your device; large videos may need more memory.
