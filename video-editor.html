Keyframe 1 defines how the text looks when it first appears. Keyframe 2 defines its appearance when the text timing ends. Pikora smoothly animates the position, size and opacity between these settings.

Text timing refers to the complete exported video timeline. Preview and export both use the keyframe settings.

5. Editing Tips

• Drag timeline clips to reorder them.

• Video and audio are displayed on separate tracks.

• Use timeline zoom to adjust the visible scale.

• The music waveform is generated in your browser.

• Select a thumbnail to preview and edit that clip.

• Use the visual trim sliders or time fields.

• Split the clip at the current preview position.

• Animate text between two keyframes.

• Export is processed on your device; large videos may need more memory.
