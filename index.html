About Pikora

Pikora is being developed as a free online creative studio for photo and video editing. Our mission is to make useful creative tools accessible to everyone, without subscriptions or unnecessary barriers.

We are building Pikora step by step, with a focus on useful features, simple design, privacy and a smooth editing experience.
